Finding 01

The niche you chose has genuine volume

Whitestown is carrying 117 new-construction listings at a median asking price of $399,500. Ryan Homes alone runs nine communities there. Pulte, Arbor, Hallmark and Davis Homes all have active communities in the same few square miles.

Plenty of hyperlocal ideas fail because the locality is too small to sustain them. This one is not. The demand behind it is the I-65 corridor and the LEAP district development in Lebanon, which is why the absorption is real rather than seasonal.

How we approach this

Short version, so nothing on the call is a surprise.

We size the market before we spend in it

The sequence is always the same: work out the local scope, research the competition and the keywords, forecast what it actually costs to compete, then settle a monthly budget. The budget comes out of the data, not out of a price list.

That is why we have not quoted you a number in this document. You asked for a realistic figure, and a realistic figure has to be calculated.

We measure in your currency, not ours

Clicks and impressions are how the platforms report. They are not how you get paid. For an agent working a market with a median around $399,500, the only measures that matter are appointments and closings, and what each one cost to generate.

We set up the tracking to answer that before the first campaign goes live, not after.

We say when we do not know something

You will have noticed the missing numbers in this document. That is deliberate. We would rather hand you three findings we can evidence than ten we cannot.

The same applies on the call. If we do not know, we will tell you, and we will tell you how we would find out.

A no is a good answer

This meeting is to work out honestly whether there is a fit. If there is not, we would both rather know in thirty minutes than in three months, and you keep the research either way.

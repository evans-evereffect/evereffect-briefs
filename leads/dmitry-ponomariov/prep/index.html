2. Qualifying questions — tied to what we found (new in v2)

Every question below is tied to a specific finding elsewhere in this dossier. Write the answer straight into Deal Qualification; an unanswered question is itself a finding.

AskWhy we're asking itListen for
"Is content/marketing for the Texas locations run centrally out of Boca Raton, or does each market run its own?" This is the single most important unanswered question in the dossier — it decides whether this is a one-time corporate engagement or a per-territory offer, and neither is assumed anywhere else in this prep. A centralized answer (one engagement, bigger scope) vs. per-territory (smaller, repeatable packages, possibly per franchisee).
"Are the Texas markets franchised locations, or company-owned expansion?" The site has both a franchise-opportunities page and direct TX location pages — not resolved which model applies to the live Texas cities specifically. Franchised (marketing decisions may not be fully centralized) vs. company-owned (Dmitry likely still owns the whole content decision).
"How are you currently tracking whether your city pages are actually generating calls, versus just being live?" Tied to the SEO audit's title-tag/OG-tag mismatch — someone updated OG copy for two states but not the title tag, which suggests uneven ownership of the SEO/content side. No real measurement in place (bigger, more urgent need) vs. an existing process being improved, not introduced.
"Is there a reason the Texas push happened now, versus staying Florida-only?" Tied to the Timing finding — the live expansion is confirmed but the actual business reason behind the timing isn't known. A named trigger (acquisition, franchisee inbound demand, a specific opportunity) vs. no particular reason.
"Have you looked at what competitors are doing on YouTube in either state?" Tied to the Competitive Landscape tab — every named competitor's content presence is thin or absent. Active awareness of the gap (easier sell, he already sees the opportunity) vs. no awareness (more of an education conversation).

3. Anticipated objections

"We're a home-services company, not a media company — why would YouTube matter to us?"Point at his own competitors: none of them have built real organic content depth either, which means whoever moves first in this specific vertical keeps that advantage in every new market the same way his location pages already compound. This isn't a media play, it's the same growth logic as opening a new city page, just on a different channel.
"We already have 144 pages on our site — isn't that enough SEO work already?"Page volume and video/organic discovery are different levers. The site itself is in good technical shape (clean schema, canonical tags, full sitemap) — the gap is specifically that none of that authority shows up on video search or in local Google Business results yet, and the title tag/meta description haven't caught up to the two-state reality that the Open Graph tags already reflect.

4. Close: recommended package & next step (new in v2)

Offer a scoped, free two-state channel audit "Happy to take a look at the current channel, the Florida and Texas site sections, and the title-tag gap I mentioned, and tell you specifically what's there to build on — no cost, no obligation. If that lands well, the next step would be a proposal scoped to however your Texas marketing is actually structured, once we know that."

2. Qualifying questions — tied to what we found (new in v2)

AskWhy we're asking itListen for

"Are you already looking at anyone else for this, or is this the first real conversation about it?" Tied to the Competitive Landscape tab — no named competitor shows real content investment either. Active evaluation of a named competitor (real urgency and leverage) vs. no active search (more of an education conversation).

This isn't "you need to prove yourself," it's "you already have the proof, it's just not findable."

The whole vertical is underinvested here, which is the opening, not a reason to skip it.
"Why is EverEffect reaching out again a month later?"Direct answer, no spin: it sat on our side, not because interest cooled. Own it plainly in the open (see step 1), then move straight to scheduling.

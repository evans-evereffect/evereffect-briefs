Finding 01: the blog is a working tool sitting idle

Finding 01

Seventeen months since the last real post

The most recent substantive post is "EverEffect is a 2025 Google Premier Partner", published 5 March 2025. Sorting the blog's own sitemap by last-modified date confirms nothing newer exists.

This is not a broken template. The March 2025 post itself carries correct Article and Person schema, meaning the mechanism works when it is used. The gap is entirely cadence.

Why it is worth naming plainly: this is the identical pattern flagged on Rhoads & Rhoads earlier this year, a capable blog gone quiet. The finding holds regardless of whose site it is on.

Finding 02: real proof, no markup

Finding 02

Testimonials and a public rating, invisible to search

The homepage carries genuine, specific client testimonials under "What Our Clients Are Saying," including a client who states they have worked with EverEffect for over fifteen years. Separately, a public agency-directory profile shows a 4.8 out of 5 rating.

Neither is marked up. No aggregateRating, no Review schema appears anywhere in the homepage source, so none of this proof can surface as a star rating in a search result. The same defect class was found and flagged on every client site audited this cycle.

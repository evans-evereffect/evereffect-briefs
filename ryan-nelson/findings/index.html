Finding 01: the niche you chose has genuine volume

Finding 01

Whitestown is absorbing real new-construction inventory

Whitestown is currently carrying 117 new-construction listings at a median asking price of $399,500. Ryan Homes alone runs nine communities there. Pulte, Arbor, Hallmark and Davis Homes all have active communities inside the same few square miles.

This matters because plenty of hyperlocal ideas fail on the simplest test: the locality is too small to sustain a campaign. This one is not. The demand behind it is the I-65 corridor and the LEAP district development in Lebanon, which is why the absorption looks structural rather than seasonal.

Finding 03: one local site proves it can be done

Finding 03

There is a working model to aim at

Exactly one result on that page is neither a portal nor a national builder: indyhomes.com/whitestown/new-construction, a local agent-side site with a dedicated Whitestown new-construction page carrying its own phone number.

That is the most useful thing we found, because it means the problem is solved rather than theoretical. It shows the shape of the page that earns the click in this specific market: a geography-specific landing page, on a domain the agent controls, with the enquiry captured directly rather than handed to a portal. We are aiming at something we can point to, not guessing.

What this adds up to. The market is real, organic is closed, and there is a proven local model. Those three together mean paid search is the right instrument, that it needs a destination page you control, and that the budget question is answerable rather than a shot in the dark. What it does not tell us yet is the number, which is exactly what we go and get next.

What we deliberately did not put in this document

You asked two specific questions: what is the realistic search volume, and what is a realistic monthly budget. There are no figures for either in here, and that is deliberate.

Producing a volume estimate or a budget without pulling the actual keyword data would mean giving you a number that sounds authoritative and is not. It is the single easiest way for an agency to look confident on a first call and be wrong by a factor of three. We would rather show you the working.

What you get instead, and when. After our call we produce a written findings document containing the real search volumes for the Whitestown terms, what the competitive terms actually cost to bid on, and a monthly budget range with the reasoning shown so you can challenge it. Every figure will be sourced the same way the three findings above are.

That document is yours to keep whether or not you decide to work with us. You asked a fair question and you should have the answer to it.

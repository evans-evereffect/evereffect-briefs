The agenda for our thirty minutes

This is the discovery process we run before quoting anyone. Nothing here requires you to prepare, though the first item will go faster if you have the site link handy.

Where the site is today We will look at your Whitestown site together and talk about where it currently stands. Before we can size an ad budget we need to be clear on where the traffic is going to land and what it needs to do when it gets there.
5 min
The scope you actually want to own Whitestown specifically, or Boone and Hendricks County more broadly. This decides everything downstream, because it sets how many searches exist to compete for.
5 min
Who you are competing against for the click We will walk you through what we found looking at the market beforehand, live, and the difference between the terms builders will always outbid you on and the ones you can realistically win.
7 min
What happens when a lead comes in Where enquiries go, how quickly you can respond, and what you do with them. Advertising into a process that cannot catch the leads is the most common way this money gets wasted.
5 min
What success looks like in ninety days In your terms: appointments, or closings, or a cost per lead you would be happy with. We would rather agree the measure before anything is spent than argue about it afterwards.
5 min
What happens next and when We will tell you what we will go and pull, what you get back, and by when. No pressure to decide anything on the call.
3 min

What happens after this call

An intro call to walk through the SLA, if it makes sense If the conversation lands and you want to go ahead, the next step is a proper introduction to how we work together: the service level agreement, what is in scope, what each line costs, who does what, and the reporting rhythm. Nothing is signed before you have seen all of it in writing.
Only if you want it
Alignment before anything starts That intro call exists so there are no surprises later. We agree the scope, the measures of success and the cadence up front, and only then does anything go live.
Before any spend
A no is a good answer. You asked a fair and specific question. If the numbers we talk through on the call do not add up for you, we would both rather know in thirty minutes than in three months.

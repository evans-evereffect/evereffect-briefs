That's a narrower, cheaper ask than "build us a content program."
"This is a heavily regulated, relationship-driven business — how much does SEO/content marketing actually move the needle?"It's not replacing relationship-driven origination, it's compounding the credibility you're already building through press and the CEO series so more of the right institutional and intermediary audience finds it organically instead of only through direct outreach.

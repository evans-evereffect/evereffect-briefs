How we approach this

Short version, so nothing on the call is a surprise.

We size the market before we spend in it

The sequence is always the same: work out the local scope, research the competition and the keywords, forecast what it actually costs to compete, then settle a monthly budget. The budget comes out of the data, not out of a price list.

That is why there is no number in this document. You asked for a realistic figure, and a realistic figure has to be calculated, then discussed, not read off a page.

We measure in your currency, not ours

Clicks and impressions are how the platforms report. They are not how you get paid. For an agent selling new construction, the only measures that matter are appointments and closings, and what each one cost to generate.

We set up the tracking to answer that before the first campaign goes live, not after.

We do the homework before the call, not the pitch

We looked into your market before reaching out. We are holding what we found for the conversation itself, on purpose, so it can be a discussion rather than something you read and react to alone. You will get the full write-up right after, either way.

A no is a good answer

This meeting is to work out honestly whether there is a fit. If there is not, we would both rather know in thirty minutes than in three months, and you keep the research either way.

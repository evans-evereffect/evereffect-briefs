Three things worth knowing before we speak

All of this came from evereffect.com and public sources, read live on 10 August 2026.

Finding 01

The blog has been quiet for about seventeen months

The last real post is "EverEffect is a 2025 Google Premier Partner," published 5 March 2025. The template itself is not the problem: that post carries correct Article and Person schema. The gap is cadence, not capability.

This is the same pattern flagged on Rhoads & Rhoads earlier this year: a working blog that has gone silent.

Where this came from The blog's own sitemap, sorted by last-modified date, and the post itself, read live 10 August 2026.
Finding 02

Real testimonials, zero review schema

The homepage shows genuine, specific client testimonials, including one client who says they've worked with EverEffect for over fifteen years. A third-party directory shows a public 4.8 out of 5 rating. None of it is marked up, so none of it can surface as a star rating in a search result.

Where this came from The homepage testimonials, and the public rating on RankWatch, both read 10 August 2026. No aggregateRating or Review schema found in the homepage source.
Finding 03

What is genuinely working

The homepage itself is technically solid: a proper title tag, meta description, Open Graph tags, a canonical link, and valid WebSite schema are all present and correct. This is not a site with broken basics, it is a site with unused upside.

Where this came from Homepage source, read 10 August 2026.
One small, fixable thing spotted along the way. The About page was updated as recently as 6 August, and the newest team photo uploaded that month ships with no alt text. Two minutes to fix, worth doing while that page is already being touched.

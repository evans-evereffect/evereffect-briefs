Discovery script — tuned to what we now know

Lead with the specific finding, not the general pitch "I watched a couple episodes of Covered by NewLeaf before this call. Real substance, and you're clearly not new to being on camera. What I noticed is the view counts have actually been trending down over the last several months even as you've kept posting, which usually means it's a discoverability problem, not a content problem."
Confirm who actually owns the channel day to day Does Sean run uploads and titles himself, or does someone on his team handle publishing? The fix here is mostly metadata and structure, not new production, so whoever touches the channel today needs to be part of the conversation about who implements changes.
Ask what he actually wants out of it Is the goal client-facing (retailers and manufacturers finding New Leaf through the show), or is it more personal brand and industry visibility for Sean? The current guest list (appliance and retail leaders) suggests both, worth having him say which matters more.
Use the specific numbers as proof you looked 487 views on a 7-month-old episode versus 11 views on one from 11 days ago is a real, visible decline. Naming it accurately, not vaguely, is what separates this from a generic cold pitch.
Offer the free audit, scoped to what is already public "If it's useful, I can go deeper on specific episodes, titles, and descriptions and come back with what I'd fix first. No new video required to start."

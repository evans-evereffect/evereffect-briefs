Discovery script — tuned to what we now know

Real call flow: open with the specific finding, qualify with questions tied to what we found, handle the objections we can already see coming, then close on a named ask.

1. Open with the specific finding

Lead with the competitive gap, not a generic pitch“You already have a YouTube channel and a real content section on your site — that already puts you ahead of at least two bigger, better-funded names in your space that we checked, AVANA Capital and Stormfield Capital, neither of which appears to have one. What we found is that the technical layer underneath your site — no structured data, no social-share metadata — means AI answer engines and shared links aren't giving you full credit for what you've already built.”

2. Qualifying questions — tied to what we found (new in v2)

Every question below is tied to a specific finding elsewhere in this dossier. Ask them, then write the answer straight into Deal Qualification — an unanswered question is itself a finding, log it as "not yet asked."

AskWhy we're asking itListen for
“Is there budget already set aside for marketing or visibility work across Fast, Fasty Funding, and the other affiliated brands, or would this be a new line item?” No paid-ads presence was found for his firm this pass (unconfirmed, not run as a dedicated ad-library check) — this asks directly rather than guessing from an absence of evidence. An existing spend line somewhere in the ecosystem (real budget authority) vs. genuinely no history (a greenfield sell, longer cycle).
“With three offices — Miami, Austin, San Diego — is this something you'd decide on your own, or does anyone on the Austin or San Diego side weigh in on marketing decisions?” He's the named Founder & Principal, but the other two offices' leadership wasn't identified by name in research — don't assume sole authority. A named co-decision-maker at another office (routes the next step there too) vs. a confirmed sole owner.
“How are you currently finding out whether people are discovering you through AI tools like ChatGPT or Gemini, versus a plain Google search?” Tied directly to the missing schema/structured-data finding — there's currently no mechanism that would even surface this to him. No tracking at all (bigger, more urgent need, matches the missing-schema finding exactly) vs. some existing process we'd be improving rather than introducing.
“Is there a reason this is coming up for you right now?” Tied to the January 2026 Authority Magazine feature — he's already mid-visibility-push, worth naming that directly rather than treating this as a cold topic. Confirmation that the press feature/other visibility work is part of a deliberate push (real urgency) vs. this being an isolated, reactive reply.
“Are you looking at anyone else for this kind of work right now?” Tied to the Competitive Landscape tab — useful to know if he's shopping this specifically or just curious. Active evaluation elsewhere (real urgency and a real deadline to work against) vs. no active search (more of an education-first conversation).

3. Anticipated objections

“We already have a website and a YouTube channel — not sure what's missing.”Point to the two concrete, checked gaps: zero Open Graph tags (shared links show no preview) and zero structured data (nothing tells Google or an AI engine what kind of business this actually is, across three cities and several affiliated brands). Specific and checkable, not a generic "you need more content" line.
“We're a small, three-office shop — not sure AI-search stuff is worth the investment for us.”He already tried this himself (the YouTube channel, the Capital Insights articles) — the objection isn't really appetite, it's proof the fix is worth it. Reframe: the gap is technical execution on something he already started, not a new category of spend.

4. Close: recommended package & next step (new in v2)

Ask for a scoped technical + content auditGiven the concrete, already-identified gaps (schema, Open Graph, H1 cleanup, a real look at YouTube cadence and GBP completeness), the natural next step is a scoped AI-search-visibility audit and fix proposal covering fastcommercialcapital.com and the affiliated brand sites — not an open-ended "let's talk marketing" close. Ask for a follow-up meeting with a firm date, or send the scoped proposal directly if he wants it in writing first.
